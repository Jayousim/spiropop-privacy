SpiroPop

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

SpiroPop collects no personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases, and the app does not connect to the internet. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

SpiroPop is a drawing toy made by Moja: roll a gear inside a ring and watch a pattern appear. This policy explains what happens to information when you play SpiroPop on Android or iOS.

Information we collect

None. SpiroPop does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. We cannot see who plays the game, how they play it, or what they draw.

What is saved on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game saves a few settings on your device only:

  • the stage you were on, and which stages you have completed
  • the auto speed you chose
  • the art theme you chose (Neon or Street)

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

SpiroPop asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and it has no internet access. While Auto mode is on, the game keeps the screen awake. That needs no permission and stops when you leave Auto mode or close the app.

Children

SpiroPop is made for everyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). There are no ads, no chat, no links out of the app and no ways to share content with other people.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of SpiroPop, please contact us at the address below.

Third parties

SpiroPop contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It is built with the Unity game engine, with Unity's analytics and crash reporting switched off.

The app store you download SpiroPop from (Google Play or the Apple App Store) may collect information as part of the download, under its own privacy policy: Google, Apple. We do not receive personal information from them.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply to you, and we delete it when the conversation is over unless you ask us to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version of the game adds a new feature, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
